How the maths works

Every equation is a monic quadratic that factors over the integers. Students need both solutions. The game accepts the roots in either order; a repeated root should be entered twice.

Hot-seat rule

After each completed turn the game hides the board behind a pass screen. The steal mechanic means an incorrect answer can immediately become an opportunity for the other player.
