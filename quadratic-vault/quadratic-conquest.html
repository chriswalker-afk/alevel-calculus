How territory capture works

You may target any territory directly above, below, left or right of one you own. A correct solution captures an unfortified territory. A successful attack on a fortified enemy territory removes its shield first, so it takes a second successful attack to capture it.

Win condition

The target score depends on player count. Territory = 1 point; each crown you currently own adds another point. If nobody reaches the target after 30 questions, the highest score wins.
