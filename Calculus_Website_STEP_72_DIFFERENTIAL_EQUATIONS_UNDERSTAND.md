# Step 72 - First-Order Differential Equations: Understand

Status: Complete

Implemented Plan 37.1-37.4 only. The topic is Understand-only and stops before Step 73 assessment/modelling work.

## Learning journey

1. Key vocabulary: differential equation, first order, independent/dependent variable, separable, general/particular solution, arbitrary constant, condition and model.
2. Translate rate statements, including proportional increase/decrease and inverse proportionality, with explicit k/sign conventions.
3. Recognise separable versus non-separable structure.
4. Separate by multiplication/division and deliberately stop after a correct separated form.
5. Integrate both sides only after separation; reuse the canonical Step 71 integration-method vocabulary and combine C1/C2 into one arbitrary C.
6. Reuse FamilyOfCurvesExplorer to show a general family and how one condition selects a particular solution.
